how / it works

Somebody else's project, your own app, all in the browser.

Every card tells you the easiest place to run that project. Here's what the buttons mean.

⚡ StackBlitz

Runs JavaScript projects inside your browser tab. No account, no install. Wait for it to load and the app appears.

💻 GitHub Codespaces

A whole computer in your browser, free for 60 hours a month with a GitHub account. Works for almost any project, even on a phone or Chromebook.

📓 Colab & Binder

For Python and notebooks. Colab (Google account) gives you a free GPU for AI projects. Binder needs no sign-in at all.

🍴 Fork

Makes your own copy on GitHub. Change anything you want, then put it online for free with GitHub Pages or Vercel.

🤖 “Make it mine” prompt

Copy it and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. It walks you through running the project, changing it and putting it online, one step at a time.

📜 License

Every card says what you're allowed to do. No license means look and learn, but ask the author before reusing their code.

The list comes from github.fetch, an open-source robot that runs on GitHub's own servers. Projects are picked by stars, not checked by a person, so read before you run anything.
