AI in one page

aictuallyhelp
cheat sheet 1

What it is

An app you talk to by typing (or speaking). It reads what you write and writes back. Like texting a really smart friend who's awake 24/7.

Free apps

ChatGPTchatgpt.com
Claudeclaude.ai
Geminigemini.google.com

Sign in with your email. The free version is enough.

Great for

  • Resumes, cover letters, emails
  • Explaining letters and forms
  • Practicing interviews
  • Budgets, checklists, plans
  • Learning anything, step by step

How to talk to it

  1. Say who you are and your situation.
  2. Say what you want — be specific.
  3. Say how you want it — short, simple, in a code block.
  4. Keep going. “Shorter.” “Simpler.” “Try again.”

Golden rules

✓ Do
  • Double-check law, health, money, deadlines
  • Call before you go somewhere
  • Read it before you send it
✕ Never type
  • Social Security number
  • Bank or card numbers
  • Passwords
AI can be wrong. You're the boss.aictuallyhelp · free to copy & share

10 prompts to keep in your pocket

aictuallyhelp
cheat sheet 2

★ The big three

1. Create a markdown file about what I'm about to say, so the next bot will build it for me in one shot.

2. Write this in a code block for me.

3. What am I missing from this, and why is it important?

Everyday

4. Ask me questions one at a time until you understand what I need. Then help me.

5. Explain [THIS] like I'm new to it. Simple words, one real example.

6. Say that again, but shorter and simpler.

7. Explain this letter in plain English. What do they want, and by when?

8. Pretend you're hiring for a [JOB]. Interview me one question at a time.

9. Fix the spelling and grammar, but keep my words and my voice.

10. Double-check your answer. What might be wrong?

Markdown in one page

aictuallyhelp
cheat sheet 3

What is it?

A .md file is plain text with a few symbols that make it neat. Any phone or computer can open it. AI reads and writes it perfectly.

Why we use it

  • Clean — titles, bullets, checklists
  • Portable — paste it into any AI
  • Forever — never needs a special app
  • Handoff — one AI writes the plan, the next AI builds it

Files & folders

File = one paper. Folder = labeled envelope. Example:

My Life/
  Jobs/resume.md
  ID & Papers/checklist.md
  Goals/2026-plan.md

sheet 4 / make an email

Make a free email in 10 minutes

aictuallyhelp
cheat sheet 4

Steps

  1. Go to accounts.google.com/signup
    (or open the Gmail app → Create account)
  2. Type your first name, last name, birthday.
  3. Pick an address. Use your name — employers will see it.
    firstname.lastname@gmail.com
  4. Make a password: 3 random words + a number.
    Example style: Maple-Rocket-Harbor-42
  5. If it asks for a phone number, use yours — or ask staff for help.
  6. Skip anything “optional.” Agree. Done!

Stuck on the phone number?

Try outlook.com instead — also free.

Pro tip

Email important stuff to yourself. Your resume, your ID checklist, your plans. Then you can open them from any computer, anywhere.

Write it down

My email address
My AI app
Password hint (NOT the password)
Keep it safe
  • Never share your password — not even with staff.
  • Keep this paper in your wallet.
  • On a shared computer: log out when done.
