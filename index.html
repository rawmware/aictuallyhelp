Why this exists

Most of what you hear about AI is aimed at people who are already winning — more money, more leverage, more of the same. But the people with the hardest, deepest stories are the ones with the most to gain.

You no longer need to be a programmer to build something. You don't need a perfect resume, a clean record, or anyone's permission. You just need to be able to say what you want — and AI can help you turn it into something real.

Your past is part of your story. It doesn't have to be the whole thing.
