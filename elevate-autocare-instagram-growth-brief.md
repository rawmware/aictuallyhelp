### Key findings

1. **Reach is the bottleneck.** Reels get ~100 views vs 140 followers — almost nothing reaches strangers. Won't grow without paid support.
2. **Bursty posting.** 4 posts in 4 days (30 Sep–3 Oct), after a 10-week gap (28 Jul→30 Sep) and a 7-week gap (31 May→17 Jul).
3. **Before/after images and gloss-enhancement content perform best.**
4. **Reels are short (3–12 s) process clips** with no hook, on-screen text or trending audio.
5. **Generic hashtags** (#CarDetailing, #CarCare, #Valeting) are saturated; local tags (#DetailingIreland, #DublinDetailing, #BMWIreland) are used inconsistently.
6. **Location unclear** (Kildare vs Dublin) and no posts are location-tagged.
7. **High-value B2B content is underused.** Fleet valeting, night-shift truck cleaning, maintenance plans and the Safety Superstore job get little visibility.
8. **Weak conversion path.** No link, no booking method, no pricing, no highlights. Every CTA is "DM us".
9. **Thin trust signals.** No reviews/testimonials posted, no named packages.
